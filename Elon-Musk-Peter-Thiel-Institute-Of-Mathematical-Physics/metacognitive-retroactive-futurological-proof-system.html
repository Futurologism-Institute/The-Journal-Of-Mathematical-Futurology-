**Yes.** Here is a compact symbolic-logic formalization of Theoretical Economics viewed through a *Metacognitive Retroactive Futurological Proof System* (MRFPS) lens. ### Core Idea of MRFPS An economic proposition \(P\) is not merely true or false in the present. It is: - **Metacognitive**: the theorist monitors and revises the epistemic status of \(P\). - **Retroactive**: future outcomes can re-classify the past validity of \(P\). - **Futurological**: the system reasons about possible future economic states as first-class objects. - **Proof-theoretic**: validity is established by a formal derivation that can be updated by later evidence. ### Primitive Symbols | Symbol | Interpretation | |--------|----------------| | \(E\) | Economic state (allocation, price vector, institutional configuration, etc.) | | \(F\) | Future economic state (a primitive “Future” object) | | \(P(E)\) | Proposition about an economic state | | \(\mathcal{M}\) | Metacognitive operator (“the agent knows / monitors the status of…”) | | \(\mathcal{R}\) | Retroactive operator (“future evidence re-classifies past validity”) | | \(\prec\) | Futurological precedence (irreflexive: one future can follow from another) | | \(\vdash_{\text{MRFPS}}\) | Derivability in the MRFPS calculus | | \(\mathsf{Val}(P,t)\) | Valuation of \(P\) at time \(t\) (true / false / undetermined) | | \(\mathsf{Conf}\) | Confidence / epistemic strength (real-valued or ordinal) | ### Axioms (Minimal MRFPS for Theoretical Economics) 1. **Irreflexivity of Futures** \(\neg(F \prec F)\) 2. **Metacognitive Monitoring** \(\mathcal{M}(P) \leftrightarrow \exists c\,(\mathsf{Conf}(P)=c \land c \text{ is tracked})\) 3. **Retroactive Revaluation** \(\mathcal{R}(P,F) \;\equiv\; \bigl(\mathsf{Val}(P,t_0)=\text{undetermined} \land F \text{ realizes} \land \mathsf{Val}(P,t_1)\in\{\top,\bot\}\bigr)\) 4. **Futurological Consistency** If \(F_1 \prec F_2\) and \(P(F_1)\) is derived, then any admissible continuation must preserve the logical consequences of \(P\) unless explicitly revised by \(\mathcal{R}\). 5. **Economic Closure** Every well-formed economic proposition \(P\) about feasible allocations, equilibrium concepts, or institutional rules is admissible in the language. ### Inference Rules - **Forward Derivation** (ordinary theoretical economics) \(\Gamma \vdash P \quad\Rightarrow\quad \Gamma \vdash_{\text{MRFPS}} P\) - **Metacognitive Lifting** \(\Gamma \vdash_{\text{MRFPS}} P \quad\Rightarrow\quad \Gamma \vdash_{\text{MRFPS}} \mathcal{M}(P)\) - **Retroactive Discharge** \[ \frac{\Gamma \vdash_{\text{MRFPS}} P \qquad F \text{ is observed} \qquad \mathcal{R}(P,F)}{\Gamma \vdash_{\text{MRFPS}} \mathsf{Val}(P)=\top/\bot} \] - **Future Precedence Introduction** \[ \frac{F_1 \text{ is a coherent continuation of } E \qquad P(E)}{F_1 \prec F_2 \text{ is admissible only if } P\text{-consequences are preserved or explicitly revised}} \] ### Example Application (General Equilibrium) Let \(P \equiv\) “Walrasian equilibrium exists and is Pareto efficient under the given preference and technology assumptions.” - Classical derivation: \(\Gamma_{\text{Arrow-Debreu}} \vdash P\). - MRFPS version: 1. \(\Gamma \vdash_{\text{MRFPS}} P\) 2. \(\Gamma \vdash_{\text{MRFPS}} \mathcal{M}(P)\) (the theorist tracks the epistemic status of the existence/efficiency claim) 3. Later observation of a future institutional or behavioral regime \(F\) that violates key assumptions triggers \(\mathcal{R}(P,F)\), possibly demoting \(P\) from “universally valid” to “valid only in a restricted domain.” 4. The system records the revision: the original proof remains historically valid, but its scope is retroactively narrowed. ### Compact Schema \[ \boxed{ \begin{align*} &\text{MRFPS-Validity of } P := \\ &\quad \exists\,\Gamma,\,F\Bigl( \Gamma \vdash_{\text{MRFPS}} P \\ &\quad\land\; \mathcal{M}(P) \\ &\quad\land\; \bigl(F \text{ does not force } \mathcal{R}(P,F)\text{ to falsify } P\bigr) \Bigr) \end{align*} } \] This gives a formal skeleton that treats theoretical-economic claims as objects that are monitored, potentially revised by future states, and proved inside a system that explicitly reasons about those futures.
